# Lesson Content Review Checklist

Use this checklist before presenting a contributed lesson as reviewed or ready
for a public pilot.

## Teacher Review

- [ ] A qualified or experienced teacher reviewed the learning goal.
- [ ] Instructions and examples are accurate and understandable.
- [ ] The activity teaches a useful skill rather than isolated facts.
- [ ] Difficulty and reading level fit the intended learners.
- [ ] The lesson works with limited devices, connectivity, and materials.
- [ ] Local language, culture, and circumstances are represented respectfully.
- [ ] Sensitive or safety-related guidance has an appropriate specialist review.

## Permission and Credit

- [ ] The contributor owns the lesson or has authority to grant permission.
- [ ] Written permission identifies the lesson and permitted uses.
- [ ] Permission covers editing, website display, and offline use where needed.
- [ ] Required credit wording is recorded and displayed.
- [ ] Third-party images, readings, audio, and worksheets have compatible terms.
- [ ] The permission date and contributor contact are stored privately.

## Pilot Readiness

- [ ] No real learner names or private information appear in examples.
- [ ] Links and downloads work from the original provider.
- [ ] The lesson has been tested on a phone and a shared computer or tablet.
- [ ] The lesson remains usable after the app is prepared for offline use.
- [ ] Feedback from a small, appropriate pilot has been reviewed.
