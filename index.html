Offline setup

Before internet goes away

Prepare the browser app and external files while online. Then test everything once before using it with a learning group.

  1. Start the local server on the teacher computer.
  2. Open LearnBridge in the browser and wait for activities to load.
  3. Download textbooks, PDFs, Kiwix ZIM files, or Kolibri content separately.
  4. Open one saved file in Teaching Desk to confirm it displays.
  5. Turn off internet or Wi-Fi and refresh the page to test offline access.

External links need internet. Download outside materials first, then use Teaching Desk to open the saved files during class.

Offline safety kit

Simple guidance to review calmly.

These notes are for basic civilian safety and class privacy. They should be reviewed by trusted local teachers, protection workers, or community leaders before real use.

Learning safely

  • Use nicknames only.
  • Ask before photos or recordings.
  • Do not write private family details in notes.

Emergency calm

  • Breathe slowly together.
  • Name five safe things you can see.
  • Let learners pass if they do not want to speak.

Earthquake

  • Drop, cover, and hold on.
  • Stay away from glass and falling objects.
  • Move carefully after shaking stops.

Smoke or fire

  • Move away from fire.
  • Stay low under smoke when leaving.
  • Do not go back inside for belongings.

Strange objects

  • Do not touch unknown metal or explosive-looking objects.
  • Move away calmly.
  • Tell a trusted adult or local safety contact.

Nearby danger

  • Move away from windows.
  • Stay low if there are loud explosions nearby.
  • Follow trusted local safety instructions.
